# Credits

## Art

**Art created by Sunny**, the creator of DigiPal. Every piece is hand drawn in Procreate:

- **Digi herself**: her character design, which her 3D model is built from.
- **The app icon** (`icon.png`).
- **Digi's room** in Customize (`studio/room/digi_room_drawing.png`, textured into `digi_room.png`).
- **Outfit designs**: the sketches behind Digi's outfits, like the beret, sun hat, bow and bunny ears.
- **The dragon book cover** (`studio/book_cover.png`), on the book Digi carries on game night.

Please don't reuse Sunny's art outside DigiPal without asking her first.

## Sounds (Calm tab)

- **White noise, pink noise**: made from scratch for Digi (`studio/make_sounds.py`).
- **Forest**: "forestday_bw66" by sonidosreales245, from
  [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/sonidosreales245/sounds/517042/) (Creative Commons 0).
  Trimmed, the loudest bird calls softened, made into a seamless loop and volume-matched.
- **Waves**: "Tayrona Colombia waves shoreline 1" by Jojo1298, from
  [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/Jojo1298/sounds/698911/) (Creative Commons 0).
  Trimmed to a calm section, made into a seamless loop and volume-matched.
- **River**: "Saliouse river murmur 1" by brunoauzet, from
  [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/brunoauzet/sounds/730154/) (Creative Commons 0).
  Trimmed, made into a seamless loop and volume-matched.

- **Chimes** (soft bell, singing bowl, Shurong bell, wind chimes, gong, tingsha, middle and end tones): made from
  scratch for Digi (`studio/make_chimes.py`).

CC0 sounds don't require credit, but thank you to the people who recorded them!
